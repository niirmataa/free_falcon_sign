## Mathematical boundary

S17 is the reference with the documented counterexample to the fixed Ext0
shortness lemma. The L_RHO candidate has verifier SHA-256
`3fe78f8df8003b760a21f4897b44b876717e30029bed031ee7d0cd224e968d42`
and is retained separately in the proof archive. This integration does not
apply that patch to `Extra/c` or change any historical proof verdict.

The focused build check uses finite public FPEMU arithmetic cases and the
existing synthetic verifier witness. It does not establish correctness of
all FPEMU operations, signing/KeyGen, full L_V or end-to-end security.

The corrected candidate now has completed L_NTT and L_V checkpoints, plus
the M0 contract and STATIC capacity proof. Those results remain tied to its
`3fe78f8d...` verifier and explicit model. This reference integration preserves
S17 exactly; the positive candidate theorem does not change the recorded
counterexample for S17. The M0 caller contract (4096-byte payload, nonce40)
is also distinct from the historical CLI (2049-byte buffer, variable rlen).
