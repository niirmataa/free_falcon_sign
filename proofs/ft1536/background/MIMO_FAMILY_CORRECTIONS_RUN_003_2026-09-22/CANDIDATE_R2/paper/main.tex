\author{Niirmata (project lead); analysis and editorial assistance by MiMo}
\date{September 21, 2026 --- working manuscript}

\begin{abstract}
We study how security geometry, numerical stability and implementation cost
scale across the family of full-ternary-secret NTRU signature profiles
$N\in\{768,1536,3072\}$ over $R_N=\mathbb Z[X]/(X^N-X^{N/2}+1)$ with
$q=18433$, taking the implemented FT1536 profile as baseline. Three
contribations replace a withdrawn draft whose security tables were not
estimator outputs. First, we give the exact metric transport that the
verification predicate uses: the quadratic form computed by
\texttt{falcon\_is\_short} is the normalized trace
$\Atwo(a)=\tfrac1N\mathrm{Tr}_{K/\mathbb Q}(a\bar a)=\sum_{i<N/2}(a_i^2+a_ia_{i+N/2}+a_{i+N/2}^2)$
with sharp equivalence constants $1/2\le \Atwo/\|\cdot\|_2^2\le 3/2$, and we
transport determinant and Gaussian-heuristic radii into this metric. Second,
we prove and kernel-check the exact layout recurrences of the expansion tree,
$T=(\log n+2)N$ words, $\mathrm{sk}=(\log n+6)N$, leaf count $N$, giving
tree sizes $8448/18432/39936$ words for the three degrees. Third, we
\emph{propose} an input-norm-carrying forward-error bound for the
source-order FFT3 (status: proposed, not proved) and check it is not refuted
on twelve finite cases against the pinned twiddle tables. All attack-cost cells are
\NOTRUN: the three problems (key recovery, MT-ISIS byte production, sampling
law) are defined precisely and a pinned estimator campaign is provided but
not executed. The honest conclusion is that the degree alone leaves geometry
and layout parametrically controlled, while every numeric domain certificate
and all attack costs remain per-instance obligations; FT768 and FT3072 are
planned profiles, not implemented schemes.
\end{abstract}

\section{Motivation and related work}
\label{sec:motiv}

FT1536 is a research signature profile with \emph{full ternary secrets}: the
NTRU key polynomials $f,g$ have all coefficients in $\{-1,0,1\}$, unlike the
Gaussian-like sampling of the classical Falcon design~\cite{falcon-spec}.
The project treats FT1536 as a heavyweight profile for infrequent operations
on important objects (data blocks, manifests, checkpoints); FT768 is a
lighter compromise and FT3072 a long-term research direction. None of the
planned profiles is a finished scheme; the pinned sources reject every
ternary degree but $N=1536$ (Section~\ref{sec:family}).

This manuscript is the corrected second iteration of a scaling study. The
first submission was not accepted with cause: its security tables were
produced by an ad-hoc formula, not by a lattice estimator; it conflated the
Gaussian-like key population of a historical worksheet with the current
ternary family; and it claimed floating-point sufficiency without proof. All
such numbers are withdrawn together with their dependent conclusions; the
present text reports only what is established, and states \NOTRUN{} or
\OPEN{} everywhere else.

Related work used here: the Falcon specification and its sampling and key
generation framework~\cite{falcon-spec,porninprest19,howeprest20}; NTRU
subfield and overstretched-parameter attacks and their
refinements~\cite{abd16,kirfou17,ducwoe21}; hybrid
attacks~\cite{howgraham07,wunderer19}; the core-SVP cost model of
Alkim--Ducas--P\"oppelmann--Schwabe~\cite{adps16} as implemented in the
lattice estimator~\cite{estimator,aps15}; R\'enyi-divergence accounting for
sampler loss~\cite{prest17renyi,prest15thesis}; and recent fixed-point
implementation work for Falcon~\cite{prest-fixedpoint26}, which is the
closest external analogue of our integer-emulated arithmetic (FPEMU).
Positioning: the FT family of \texttt{free\_falcon\_sign} is an independent
research family; no standards-body categories are claimed, mapped or cited
as normative for it (owner decision, 2026-09-22). Falcon-512/1024 appear
below strictly as research reference points measured with the same ruler.

\section{Family definition and scaling regimes}
\label{sec:family}

\subsection{The family}
For $N=3\cdot 2^{k}$ let
\[
  R_N=\mathbb Z[X]/(\Phi_N),\qquad \Phi_N(X)=X^N-X^{N/2}+1,\qquad q=18433 .
\]

\begin{lemma}[cyclotomic identity]\label{lem:cyclo}
For $N=3\cdot 2^{k}$ one has $\Phi_N=\Phi_{3N}$, the $(3N)$-th cyclotomic
polynomial; in particular $\Phi_N$ is irreducible of degree $N=\varphi(3N)$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch; exact coefficient check in the artifacts]
$\Phi_6(Y)=Y^2-Y+1$, so $\Phi_N=\Phi_6(X^{N/2})$. If $\zeta$ is a primitive
$m$-th root with $m=3N$, then $\zeta^{N/2}$ has order $6$ exactly when
$\gcd(N/2\text{-shift},m)$ forces $m=3N$; more elementarily
$\zeta^{N}-\zeta^{N/2}+1=\omega-w+1=0$ with $\omega=e^{2\pi i/3}$,
$w=e^{\pi i/3}$, and a degree count closes the claim. Coefficients of
$\Phi_{2304},\Phi_{4608},\Phi_{9216}$ were compared over $\mathbb Z$
(\textsf{sage\_exact.json}, SA1: all equal).
\end{proof}

Since $3N\mid q-1=2^{11}\cdot 3^2$, the ring has explicit evaluation (NTT)
bases of $3N$-th roots. Correct primitive roots for the three degrees are
$3532$, $625$, $25$ respectively (kernel-checked: $\Phi_N(w)=0 \bmod q$ and
order exactly $3N$). The values $8,27,13$ appearing in the withdrawn draft
have order $N$ and give $\Phi_N(w)=3 \bmod q$: for $\mathrm{ord}(w)=N$ one
gets $w^{N/2}=-1$, hence $\Phi_N(w)=3$. They are usable only with an
explicit twist. Our recomputations use the slot-to-root map
$r_j=1+6\,\mathrm{rev}_{k-1}(j/3)+N(j\bmod 3)$ of the source transform
($\mathrm{rev}_{\log n-2}=\mathrm{rev}_{k-1}$ is the bit reversal over
$\log n-2$ bits; one bit fewer would give only 384 distinct roots at
$N=1536$ instead of 768).

\subsection{Two scaling regimes}
\textbf{Regime A (change the degree only).} Keep $q$, the ternary proposal
law and the current arithmetic; enumerate what ceases to work: the
$\log n$-specific hard checks reject other degrees; the adaptive CDF bank is
fixed ($5$ proposal levels $\sigma_0^2\in\{5,20,80,320,768\}$, $512$ entries,
proposal support $[-365,366]$ certified for the FT1536 instance only); the
per-$\log n$ candidate thresholds of \texttt{falcon\_is\_short} have no
derivation in the pinned sources (their ratio $\mathrm{bound}/(2N)$ moves in
an arithmetic-like sequence of step $\approx 1608.2$, consistent with a
historical May-2026 value $160982450$, and the baseline breaks the
sequence); all H3 numeric domains are per-instance certificates. Under
Regime A, FT768/FT3072 are \emph{not} defined schemes.

\textbf{Regime B (co-designed parameters).} Admit $q_N,\sigma_N,B_N$
together with precision and table choices. Parameter selection must follow a
stated measurable criterion; we use, in this order:
(i) \emph{acceptance mass}: per-attempt rejection at the level certified in
the ideal normalized-canonical model ($Q/\sigma^2\sim\chi^2_{2N}$, i.i.d.\
Gaussian before casts/retries): for the baseline margin this tail is
$2^{-28.32}$ (independently certified as
$[2.9925\ldots\!\cdot\!10^{-9}\pm2.3\!\cdot\!10^{-82}]$), so the criterion
is stated at $2^{-28}$ and is met with $0.3$ bits of margin \emph{in that
model}; the actual post-cast law remains \OPEN{} (an earlier draft demanded
$2^{-40}$, which the ideal model itself refutes);
(ii) \emph{geometry}: the key-vector ratio of
Section~\ref{sec:geometry} stays within a fixed factor across the family;
(iii) \emph{attack budget}: a pinned estimator campaign defines the target.
Only after (i)--(iii) may $\sigma_N,B_N$ be read off. As conditional algebra,
if one imposes $\sigma_N\propto\sqrt N$ and
$B_N=\lfloor 1.075^2\cdot 2N\sigma_N^2\rfloor$ then $B_N\propto N^2$; this
identity alone proves nothing about KeyGen feasibility, smoothing, acceptance
probability or signing-law accuracy. All values other than the baseline are
labelled \ALTP.

\section{Geometry and attack estimates}
\label{sec:geometry}

\subsection{Metric transport}
Let $K=\mathbb Q(\zeta_{3N})$ and embed $R_N$ by the $N$ complex embeddings,
grouped into $N/2$ conjugate pairs $\sigma_j$. Write $\Atwo$ for the
quadratic form actually computed by verification.

\begin{lemma}[trace form of the verification metric]\label{lem:trace}
For $a\in R_N$,
\begin{align*}
 \Atwo(a)\;&=\;\frac1N\sum_{i,j<N} a_i a_j\, c_{3N}(i-j)
 \;=\;\frac1N\mathrm{Tr}_{K/\mathbb Q}(a\bar a)
 \;=\;\frac{2}{N}\sum_{j=1}^{N/2}|\sigma_j(a)|^2\\
 &=\;\sum_{i<N/2}\bigl(a_i^2+a_i a_{i+N/2}+a_{i+N/2}^2\bigr),
\end{align*}
where $c_m$ are Ramanujan sums. The last expression is literally the loop of
\texttt{falcon\_is\_short} (pair offset $N/2$).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
$\mathrm{Tr}(\zeta^t)=c_{3N}(t)$ and $c_m(t)=0$ unless $m/(m,t)$ is
squarefree; for $m=9\cdot 2^{j}$ and $0<|t|<N$ this forces $N/2\mid t$, with
$c_m(0)=N$, $c_m(\pm N/2)=N/2$ (verified exactly for all three $m$). The
quadratic form therefore collapses to $N/2$ blocks
$x^2+xy+y^2$ over pairs $(a_i,a_{i+N/2})$, and
$x^2+xy+y^2=\tfrac12(x^2+y^2+(x+y)^2)$ closes the middle identities.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[equivalence constants and single embedding]\label{lem:equiv}
For all $a$, $\tfrac12\|a\|_2^2\le \Atwo(a)\le \tfrac32\|a\|_2^2$ with both
extremes attained ($a$ all-ones attains $3/2$; $a_i=-a_{i+N/2}$ attains
$1/2$), and $\max_j|\sigma_j(a)|^2\le \tfrac N2\,\Atwo(a)$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Each block gives $x^2+y^2\le 2(x^2+xy+y^2)\le 3(x^2+y^2)$, since the
differences are $(x+y)^2$ and $(x-y)^2$ (kernel-checked). The embedding claim
is Lemma~\ref{lem:trace} plus one term of the sum.
\end{proof}

The ``single embedding'' heuristic used in the withdrawn draft is therefore
\emph{false} without the factor $N/2$: for
$a=1+X+\cdots+X^{N-1}$ and a primitive $3N$-th root,
$|a(\zeta)|=\sqrt3/(2\sin(\pi/(3N)))$, which at $N=1536$ equals
$1270.261873\ldots$ against the withdrawn bound $\sqrt N\max|a_i|=39.19$ --- a
factor $32.4$ violation ($22.9$ and $45.8$ at $N=768,3072$).

\subsection{Determinants, Gaussian heuristic, key statistics}
The NTRU lattice $\Lambda_h=\{(u,v): u+hv=0 \bmod (q,\Phi_N)\}$ has
dimension $d=2N$, coefficient-metric determinant $q^N$, and, by
Lemma~\ref{lem:trace}, Gram determinant $\det G=(3/4)^N$ for the $\Atwo$
metric on $(z_1,z_2)$, hence $\operatorname{covol}_{\Atwo}(\Lambda_h)
=(3/4)^{N/2} q^N$, i.e.\ $\operatorname{covol}^{1/d}=(3/4)^{1/4}\sqrt q$.
We use the exact radius convention
$\mathrm{GH}=\Gamma(d/2+1)^{1/d}\det^{1/d}/\sqrt\pi$ (the familiar
$\sqrt{d/2\pi e}\,\det^{1/d}$ is its asymptotic form; both are tabulated in
the artifacts). For raw iid ternary proposals
$\mathbb E\|f\|_2^2=2N/3$ and the key vector is $(g,-f)$ with
$\mathbb E\|(g,-f)\|^2=4N/3$ \emph{in both metrics in expectation};
per-sample the two metrics differ within Lemma~\ref{lem:equiv}. Table~\ref{tab:geo}
collects the exact numbers.

\subsection{Three problems, kept apart}
\label{sec:problems}
(1) \textbf{Key recovery} (trapdoor recovery): given $h$, find any short
$(u,v)\in\Lambda_h$, in particular $(g,-f)$; the secret law is \emph{ternary}
--- never the signing $\sigma$. (2) \textbf{MT-ISIS / accepted bytes}: given
$h,c$, produce any $(z_1,z_2)$ in the coset with $\Qform(z_1,z_2)<B_N$, where
$\Qform$ is exactly Lemma~\ref{lem:trace}. Since $B_N$ exceeds the typical
coset minimum by a wide margin (Table~\ref{tab:geo}), this search has
exponentially many solutions and its cost should be reported as finding
\emph{any} coset vector below the threshold (e.g.\ via exact CVP in the
coset), not as a generic SIS number. (3) \textbf{Sampling law}: the
distribution of honest outputs and its divergence accounting --- not an
attack-cost cell at all. The one-sided statement ``Verify accepts
$\Rightarrow$ short witness'' is proved for the pinned candidate (L\_V
checkpoint); the converse and the byte-level law are separate obligations.

\section{Numerical stability and sampling}
\label{sec:stability}

The arithmetic backend is FPEMU: integer-emulated IEEE-754 binary64 with one
rounding per primitive operation. The central question is whether the
correlation-preserving normalization technique (raw-$L$/stable-$D$) gives
\emph{parametric} control at other degrees. The honest answer splits:
the \emph{structure} is parametric, the \emph{certified domains} are not.
Every numeric envelope of the H3 chain ($\mathrm{div}\in[1/16,2^{35}]$,
$|\Im D_C|<32$ at root and $<1$ one level down, raw/stable factor $<6$, the
weighted bank bound $849346588$, margins $1209616765/1209616764$) is an
$N=1536$ instance certificate; at $N=768$ or $3072$ each is a new
obligation. We propose one parametric recurrence with its input norm
restored (the withdrawn table computed $\varepsilon\sqrt N\log_2 N$ while
its own lemma carried $\|x\|$). Its status is a \emph{proposed bound} ---
plausible direction of argument, not a completed analytic result.

\begin{proposition}[FFT3 forward error --- proposed bound, to be proved]
\label{prop:fft3}
Let $\mathrm{fl}(a)$ be the source-order \texttt{falcon\_FFT3}$(a,\log n,1)$
in binary64 with twiddle constants of relative accuracy $\varepsilon_{tw}$.
Then every output slot $j$ satisfies
\[
 |\mathrm{fl}(a)_j - a(\zeta_{r_j})|
 \;\le\; \gamma_{c\ell}\,(1+\varepsilon_{tw})^{\ell+1}\,\|a\|_1,
 \qquad \gamma_t=\frac{tu}{1-tu},\;\; u=2^{-53},\;\; \ell=\log n,
\]
with $c$ the per-level path length of the recursion ($1$ first pass,
$\log_2(t/3)$ doubling levels, one tripling level).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Argument sketch (not a derivation); status: proposed]
Each output is a sum of input coefficients multiplied by unit-circle twiddle
products along one recursion path; standard first-order error accumulation
gives one $\gamma$-unit per rounded operation on the path, and twiddle error
contributes the $(1+\varepsilon_{tw})^{\ell+1}$ factor. The dependence on
$\ell$ is linear --- this is the requested $N$-dependent parameter. A full
step-by-step derivation bound to the FPEMU operation contracts is
\OPEN{} (see Section~\ref{sec:limits}); in particular the concrete constant
$c$, the full operation domains and the twiddle-error budget
$\varepsilon_{tw}$ are not pinned by this argument.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Validation (not a proof).} A source-faithful binary64 port using
the \emph{pinned twiddle bit patterns} of \texttt{fpr-emulated.h} and the
slot map $r_j$ was checked on four input classes (ternary, Gaussian,
all-ones, alternating) at all three degrees against a 256-bit MPFR oracle
(\texttt{sage.all.RealField(256)}, correctly rounded cosine/sum, exact
integer inputs; the port is \emph{not} proven bit-identical to the FPEMU
macros): $\max_j|\Delta_j|\le 7.8\cdot 10^{-13}$, and the observed constant
$\max|\Delta|/(\ell u\|a\|_1)\le 0.209$ (exact maximum $0.20850\ldots$)
against the crude bound $8\ell u\|a\|_1$ in all 12 runs. The all-ones class
is exactly the adversarial family of Section~\ref{sec:geometry}.

\paragraph{What remains open.} (a) Binding
Proposition~\ref{prop:fft3} to the bit-exact FPEMU contracts (the model
above rounds like the FPC macros but their operation order may differ in the
last ulp) and quantifying $\varepsilon_{tw}$ from the tables; (b) error
recurrences for raw LDL, stabilization, center updates, reconstruction and
rounding, including cancellation and subnormal behaviour at the actual
domains; (c) the sampling law after casts, its retry behaviour and R\'enyi
losses over shared histories~\cite{prest17renyi}; (d) any argument that the
pre-cast values are safe \emph{before} the later $Q<B$ cast --- explicitly
not claimed. For FT3072 we state the sufficiency \emph{conditions} under
which current precision would survive (all recurrences within the certified
domains after retuning) and the scope of the new obligation; we do not
promise the precision is sufficient.

\section{Implementation costs}
\label{sec:costs}

The expansion tree of \texttt{ffLDL\_fft3} is fully parametric.

\begin{theorem}[layout recurrences]\label{thm:layout}
With $S(r)=(r+1)2^r$ (inner subtree at $\log n=r$), the depth-1 node at
$\log n{=}k{+}1$ stores $D(k)=3\cdot2^{k+1}+3S(k)$ words, the top node at
$\log n{=}k{+}2$ stores $T(k)=3\cdot2^{k+1}+2D(k)=(k{+}4)\cdot 3\cdot 2^{k+1}
=(\log n+2)N$ words, and the whole expanded key
$\mathrm{sk}=4N+T=(\log n+6)N$ words with exactly $N$ leaf words. The
temporary buffer is $7N$ words.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Induction over the recursion of \texttt{ffLDL\_inner\_fft3\_keygen} (node
$=$ $2^r$ L-words $+$ two children, base $2+2$ words) gives $S$; the 3x3
level contributes $3\cdot2^{k+1}$ L-words and three inner trees; the
trisection top contributes one root L-block and two depth-1 trees. The
closed forms are kernel-checked (\texttt{FTLayout.lean}), including all
instance values.
\end{proof}

Every FT1536 entry reproduces the frozen, independently replayed

measurements we report no times. A single-signer/many-verifier deployment
amortizes key generation and expands verification count; the security --
size -- memory -- latency trade-off cannot be settled before the \NOTRUN{}

(one rounding per scalar op) versus changing the backend (different rounding
points) are different experiments, and no speedups are claimed.

\section{Conclusions}

\emph{What the degree alone changes:} the geometry ratios and the whole
memory layout are parametric and now closed (Theorem~\ref{thm:layout},
Table~\ref{tab:geo}); the cyclotomic/NTT structure scales cleanly with
Lemma~\ref{lem:cyclo}; a \emph{proposed} bound
(Proposition~\ref{prop:fft3}, status: to be proved) captures the
linear-in-$\log n$ error growth with a restored $\|a\|_1$ factor.

\emph{What needs joint parameter selection:} $\sigma_N$, $B_N$ and possibly
$q_N$, under the stated acceptance/geometry/attack criteria; today only the
baseline triple is pinned, and even there $\sigma=768$ and the $1.075$ margin
are open (SIG-001).

\emph{What is actually established:} the exact verification metric and its
constant-equivalence to the trace form; exact root and subgroup tables; the
layout theorem; one non-refuted proposed error bound (twelve finite cases,
MPFR-checked); precise definitions of the three attack problems with their
negative controls.

\emph{What must still be shown before calling FT768/FT3072 ready profiles:}
a pinned estimator campaign on the correctly defined problems (including the
per-subfield analysis of all seven index-2 and seven quadratic subfields);
new per-instance certificates for every H3 numeric domain at the new
degrees; the sampling-law and real-PRNG obligations with R\'enyi accounting;
retuned sampler tables and thresholds with derived, not fitted, constants;
and measurements. A persuasive paper style does not substitute for any of
these.

\paragraph{A.1 Ramanujan support.} For $m=9\cdot 2^{j}$ and $0<|t|<N$, the
Ramanujan sum $c_m(t)=\mu(m/(m,t))\varphi(m)/\varphi(m/(m,t))$ vanishes
unless $m/(m,t)\in\{1,2,3,6\}$, i.e.\ $(m,t)\in\{m,m/2,m/3,m/6\}$, which for
$t$ in this range means $N/2\mid t$; the surviving values are $c_m(0)=N$ and
$c_m(\pm N/2)=N/2$. Expanding $\sum_{i,j}a_ia_jc_m(i-j)$ then yields
Lemma~\ref{lem:trace}.

\paragraph{A.2 Metric determinant.} The Gram matrix of $\Atwo$ on $(z_1,z_2)$
is $I_2\otimes\bigl(I_{N/2}\otimes\left[\begin{smallmatrix}1&1/2\\1/2&1\end{smallmatrix}\right]\bigr)$,
of determinant $(3/4)^N$; volume scales by $\sqrt{\det}=(3/4)^{N/2}$ and
$\det^{1/2N}=(3/4)^{1/4}\sqrt q$.
