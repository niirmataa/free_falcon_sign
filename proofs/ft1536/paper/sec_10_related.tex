Falcon's specification~\cite{falcon2020} supplies the immediate design
and implementation context. This draft compares concrete probability
laws and named proof interfaces rather than inferring a security
reduction from a shared design lineage. The artifact also records
model-dependent estimator outputs; a comparison with published
estimator conventions must preserve both the attack model and the
classical/quantum cost units.\src{C13,C14}
Falcon~\cite{falcon2018,falcon2020} instantiates the GPV hash-and-sign
framework~\cite{gpv2008} over NTRU lattices~\cite{hps1998};
trapdoor-free lattice signatures~\cite{lyu2012} and the Dilithium
design~\cite{dilithium2018} occupy the neighboring Fiat--Shamir space.
The Gram--Schmidt-norm key-recovery line~\cite{fktwy2020} is the kind
of leakage analysis that Assumption~\ref{ass:a5} places outside this
paper's game. These citations provide design and attack context only;
they are not statements of comparable guarantees.
