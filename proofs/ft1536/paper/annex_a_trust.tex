\subsection{What each layer establishes}
The artifact has four distinct checks. A SHA-256 pin identifies bytes;
a job receipt relates inputs, command, exit status and outputs; a Lean
kernel check establishes the stated proposition under its dependencies;
and a review checks whether that proposition matches the claimed scope.
None of these steps is interchangeable with the others. In particular,
matching hashes do not prove a mathematical claim, and a successful
build does not prove that a premise was meaningfully used.\src{C17}

The companion \code{SOURCES.md} maps each source identifier in this
draft to exact repository paths and declaration or receipt names.
\code{SOURCES.sha256} pins those files, including the archived copies
used through \code{ARCHIVED_DEPENDENCIES.json}. Live-development paths
are interpreted at the recorded snapshot, not at an unspecified future
HEAD. The paper's build products are confined to \code{paper/build/}.
\src{C17}

\subsection{Axiom audits and the review record}
The corrected computational package records Lean $4.34.0$, with Lean
and Mathlib revisions in its environment record. Its API audit has
$79$ distinct names: $63$ in \code{CompPrg} and $16$ in
\code{AssemblyComp}. These include structure constructors, recursors
and projections; they are not $79$ theorems. The historical $64$-name
audit concerns the earlier source version. The corrected audit reports
only subsets of \code{propext}, \code{Classical.choice} and
\code{Quot.sound}. These are recorded source/audit results, not a new
Lean replay performed while typesetting this paper.\src{C17}

The review chronology matters:
\begin{enumerate}
 \item REVIEW\_001 reports an independent fresh-context review of the
 earlier computational package, a $70/70$-step replay, and
 \code{CHANGES_REQUIRED} for the experiment, cost and key-law defects.
 \item The corrections were committed at \code{6ff3c838}.
 REVIEW\_002 reports $70/70$ final base steps and a technical
 \code{PASS_SCOPED}, but explicitly identifies itself as
 \code{AUTHOR_RECHECK}, with independence and fresh-context flags false.
 \item Its $k=1$/inverse supplement adds a clean accepted control step;
 the final result records $71$ successful selected steps. The supplement
 does not change the author's lack of independence from the correction.
 \item The subsequently delivered REVIEW\_003 records an independent,
 fresh-context \code{PASS_SCOPED} for the corrected package. It reports
 $66$ rebuilt closure modules and two API audits ($68$ successful selected
 steps), $79$ audited API names, and $21$ additional kernel control
 declarations. Its result explicitly leaves the honest-Sign bridge,
 concrete key/generator laws, machine-cost model and A2/A3/A4 open.
\end{enumerate}
The synthesis also records older reviews of pre-correction snapshots.
Review roles and acceptance are version-specific: the author's recheck
is not another independent review, and the independent public-game
verdict is not acceptance of the final implementation claim.
Archive import and the final acceptance/tag procedure remain separate
from the recorded review verdict.\src{C17}

\subsection{Counterexamples and retained failures}
Review controls test semantic consumption as well as compilation:
class membership without a valid cost line, a proposition without
key-law equality, and $D=0$ without a local sampler certificate.
The negative results of Section~\ref{sec:sampler} use explicit
kernel witnesses. Failed parser bindings and unsuccessful proof or
audit attempts are retained in the recorded jobs rather than replaced
by a clean-looking history. A later successful receipt identifies the
selected final result.\src{C09,C12,C17}

\subsection{Reproduction and release availability}\label{app:release}
\paragraph{Validation (not a proof).}
From \code{proofs/ft1536/paper}, \code{make check} compiles the PDF,
checks the cited-byte pins, source identifiers and final TeX log.
It also checks that the generated snapshot identifiers occur in the
PDF. From the repository root, the direct manifest check is
\begin{quote}\small
\path{sha256sum -c proofs/ft1536/paper/SOURCES.sha256}.
\end{quote}
The manifest paths are root-relative. Neither command refreshes pins;
an input change must be reconciled explicitly before a new snapshot.
\code{make numbers} runs a byte-identical runtime copy of
\code{tools/check_numbers.sage} as \code{sage check_numbers.sage}
with the standard preparser;
it records its source pins, command and outputs under \code{build/}.
This arithmetic check is not a replacement for the general Lean
proofs or for the S06 campaign.\src{C20}

Some receipts and reviews cited here reside in ignored \code{work/}
or \code{.build/} directories. Local availability and a hash do not
establish that those bytes are recoverable from a public Git clone.
The release must provide their archival locations, the proof dependency
closure and the corresponding replay commands. This draft therefore
does not claim that every cited runtime artifact is already publicly
downloadable.\src{C17}
