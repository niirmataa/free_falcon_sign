Motivation and related work

FT1536 is a research signature profile with full ternary secrets: the NTRU key polynomials
f, g have all coefficients in {−1, 0, 1}, unlike the Gaussian-like sampling of the classical Falcon
design [7]. The project treats FT1536 as a heavyweight profile for infrequent operations on
important objects (data blocks, manifests, checkpoints); FT768 is a lighter compromise and
FT3072 a long-term research direction. None of the planned profiles is a finished scheme; the
pinned sources reject every ternary degree but N = 1536 (Section 2).
This manuscript is the corrected second iteration of a scaling study. The first submission
was not accepted with cause: its security tables were produced by an ad-hoc formula, not by
a lattice estimator; it conflated the Gaussian-like key population of a historical worksheet with
the current ternary family; and it claimed floating-point sufficiency without proof. All such
numbers are withdrawn together with their dependent conclusions; the present text reports
only what is established, and states NOT_RUN or OPEN everywhere else.
Related work used here: the Falcon specification and its sampling and key generation
framework [7, 11, 8]; NTRU subfield and overstretched-parameter attacks and their refinements [1, 10, 6]; hybrid attacks [9, 14]; the core-SVP cost model of Alkim–Ducas–Pöppelmann–
Schwabe [4] as implemented in the lattice estimator [2, 3]; Rényi-divergence accounting for
sampler loss [13, 12]; and recent fixed-point implementation work for Falcon [5], which is the
closest external analogue of our integer-emulated arithmetic (FPEMU). Positioning: the FT
family of free_falcon_sign is an independent research family; no standards-body categories

Three problems, kept apart

(1) Key recovery (trapdoor recovery): given h, find any short (u, v) ∈ Λh , in particular
(g, −f ); the secret law is ternary — never the signing σ. (2) MT-ISIS / accepted bytes:
given h, c, produce any (z1 , z2 ) in the coset with Q(z1 , z2 ) < BN , where Q is exactly Lemma 2.
Since BN exceeds the typical coset minimum by a wide margin (Table 2), this search has
exponentially many solutions and its cost should be reported as finding any coset vector below
the threshold (e.g. via exact CVP in the coset), not as a generic SIS number. (3) Sampling
law: the distribution of honest outputs and its divergence accounting — not an attack-cost
cell at all. The one-sided statement “Verify accepts ⇒ short witness” is proved for the pinned
candidate (L_V checkpoint); the converse and the byte-level law are separate obligations.

Numerical stability and sampling

The arithmetic backend is FPEMU: integer-emulated IEEE-754 binary64 with one rounding per
primitive operation. The central question is whether the correlation-preserving normalization
technique (raw-L/stable-D) gives parametric control at other degrees. The honest answer splits:
the structure is parametric, the certified domains are not. Every numeric envelope of the H3
chain (div ∈ [1/16, 235 ], |ℑDC | < 32 at root and < 1 one level down, raw/stable factor < 6, the
weighted bank bound 849346588, margins 1209616765/1209616764) is an N = 1536 instance
certificate; at N = 768 or 3072 each is a new obligation. We propose
one parametric recurrence

not by itself carry over to ideal phases.
Validation (not a proof ). A source-faithful binary64 port using the pinned twiddle

(ternary, Gaussian, all-ones, alternating) at all three degrees against a 256-bit MPFR oracle
(sage.all.RealField(256), correctly rounded cosine/sum, exact integer inputs; the port is
not proven bit-identical to the FPEMU macros): maxj |∆j | ≤ 7.8 · 10−13 , and the observed
constant max |∆|/(ℓu∥a∥1 ) ≤ 0.209 (exact maximum 0.20850 . . .) against the crude bound
8ℓu∥a∥1 in all 12 runs. The all-ones class is exactly the adversarial family of Section 3. Twelve
finite cases establish neither a uniform constant c nor the operation domains nor any FPEMU
contract; they only record that the proposed bound is not refuted on them.
What remains open. (a) Binding Proposition 1 to the bit-exact FPEMU contracts (the
model above rounds like the FPC macros but their operation order may differ in the last ulp)
and quantifying εtw from the tables; (b) error recurrences for raw LDL, stabilization, center
updates, reconstruction and rounding, including cancellation and subnormal behaviour at the
actual domains; (c) the sampling law after casts, its retry behaviour and Rényi losses over
shared histories [13]; (d) any argument that the pre-cast values are safe before the later Q < B
cast — explicitly not claimed. For FT3072 we state the sufficiency conditions under which
current precision would survive (all recurrences within the certified domains after retuning)
and the scope of the new obligation; we do not promise the precision is sufficient.

Implementation costs

The expansion tree of ffLDL_fft3 is fully parametric.
Theorem 1 (layout recurrences). With S(r) = (r + 1)2r (inner subtree at log n = r), the
depth-1 node at log n=k+1 stores D(k) = 3 · 2k+1 + 3S(k) words, the top node at log n=k+2
stores T (k) = 3 · 2k+1 + 2D(k) = (k+4) · 3 · 2k+1 = (log n + 2)N words, and the whole expanded
key sk = 4N + T = (log n + 6)N words with exactly N leaf words. The temporary buffer is 7N
words.
Proof sketch. Induction over the recursion of ffLDL_inner_fft3_keygen (node = 2r L-words
+ two children, base 2 + 2 words) gives S; the 3x3 level contributes 3 · 2k+1 L-words and three
inner trees; the trisection top contributes one root L-block and two depth-1 trees. The closed
forms are kernel-checked (FTLayout.lean), including all instance values.

Evidence and limitations

Evidence classes are kept apart throughout, and the artifact ledger records each claim’s class
and scope. Kernel-checked (Lean 4, clean logs, no sorry/native_decide): the A2 identities and equivalence constants (Lemma 3 algebra), the layout recurrences and all word counts
(Theorem 1), the root/order tables and the threshold arithmetic B = 2093922385. Exact computation (Sage/GAP and binary64 controls): the cyclotomic identity, the Ramanujan support
of Lemma 2, trace-form instances, the subgroup census, the counterexample values and the
FFT3 validation. Analytic in this text: Lemmas 1–3, the determinant and GH transports,
Proposition 1 (sketch level). NOT_RUN: every attack cost and every timing. OPEN: sampling
law and real-PRNG bridge, pre-cast safety, precision sufficiency for FT3072, SIG-001 parameter
selection.
Hash agreement of text with any manifest is not mathematical checking; a timing measurement with no signal is not constant-time evidence. Nothing here certifies FT768/FT3072,
claims a security level, or evaluates deployment in critical infrastructure.

7

Conclusions

What the degree alone changes: the geometry ratios and the whole memory layout are parametric and now closed (Theorem 1, Table 2); the cyclotomic/NTT structure scales cleanly with
Lemma 1; a proposed bound (Proposition 1, status: to be proved) captures the linear-in-log n
error growth with a restored ∥a∥1 factor.
What needs joint parameter selection: σN , BN and possibly qN , under the stated acceptance/geometry/attack criteria; today only the baseline triple is pinned, and even there σ = 768
and the 1.075 margin are open (SIG-001).
7

What is actually established: the exact verification metric and its constant-equivalence to the
trace form; exact root and subgroup tables; the layout theorem; one non-refuted proposed error
bound (twelve finite cases, MPFR-checked); precise definitions of the three attack problems
with their negative controls.
What must still be shown before calling FT768/FT3072 ready profiles: a pinned estimator
campaign on the correctly defined problems (including the per-subfield analysis of all seven
index-2 and seven quadratic subfields); new per-instance certificates for every H3 numeric domain at the new degrees; the sampling-law and real-PRNG obligations with Rényi accounting;
retuned sampler tables and thresholds with derived, not fitted, constants; and measurements.
A persuasive paper style does not substitute for any of these.
